🗺️ Treasure Grid

A turn-based multiplayer treasure hunt

How to Play

One shared grid, hidden treasures, bombs, and power-ups. Players take turns revealing one cell at a time.

  • 💰 Treasure — bronze/silver/gold worth 1/2/3 points, and grants you a bonus turn.
  • 💣 Bomb — costs 1 point (never below 0) and ends your turn right away, unless you're shielded.
  • 👁️ Peek power-up — privately shows you what's under a random hidden cell.
  • ⏩ Extra Turn power-up — go again immediately.
  • 🛡️ Shield power-up — blocks the score loss from your next bomb.
  • 🍓 Warmth hint — a revealed empty cell shows 1-3 strawberries based on how close the nearest treasure is.
  • ⏱️ Each turn has 15 seconds — this drops to 6 seconds in Sudden Death once only a few treasures remain.
  • 🏆 Matches are best-of-3 rounds. Highest score wins each round; first to 2 round wins takes the match.
  • 👀 Anyone can join a room as a spectator to watch without playing.
  • 🗓️ Daily Challenge (from the home screen) is a solo puzzle with the same board for everyone each day, with a shared leaderboard.
